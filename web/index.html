    About Launch Library 2

    History starts with our first capture

    Earlier announcements cannot be reconstructed from today's record. Comparisons become available after a second capture.

    Precision belongs with the date

    NET means “no earlier than”. A month- or year-level forecast is displayed at that precision. The provider's status records an outcome; a date passing does not.

    Expand the collection

    Edit web/config.json to change the date window, provider, search or launch UUID watchlist. A config push refreshes automatically. Use Run workflow for an on-demand capture.

    Your search stays here

    Filters and imported receipts stay in this tab. The page reads only published CoolCode data and sends no launch queries to the provider. Downloads are your own copy; imports do not change the public archive. The shared CoolCode counter records aggregate tool opens.
